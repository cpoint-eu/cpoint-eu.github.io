Pet

The pet is a small animated character that sits in the corner of the conversation and shows what the agent is doing: working, thinking, waiting for you, or done. No need to follow the text, a glance at the pet is enough. It can also have a mood, comment on the work in a speech bubble and answer when you talk to it by name.

The pet belongs to Hermes, so you see the same pet in the terminal (hermes, TUI) too. Kyllene shows it and adds the mood, the comments and the personality on top. Česky: Maskot.

Turn it on and off

  1. Open Settings (gear icon in the top bar or Ctrl+,) and pick the Pet tab on the left.
  2. Switch on Show the pet. Switching it off hides the pet everywhere: in Kyllene and in the terminal.
  3. The Size slider sets how big it is (Default resets it). You can also resize it on the pet itself: hold Alt and turn the mouse wheel.

The palette does the same: Ctrl+K › Pet: show / Pet: hide. If the tab says This Hermes has no pets yet, update Hermes (hermes update).

Install and pick a pet

In the app: Settings › Pet has the Petdex at the bottom, a public gallery of pets. Search for one and click Adopt. It shows up under Your pets; Show makes it the one you see (it then says Shown). Its ⋯ menu (More) has Rename, Personality…, Repair animations… (pets you hatched), Generate moods… and Remove. To switch quickly, Ctrl+K › Pet… lists your pets with search; Enter shows the one you pick.

In a terminal: one command downloads a pet and picks it, for example Boba:

hermes pets install boba --select

More: hermes pets list (the gallery), hermes pets select <name> (switch to another), hermes pets off (turn it off). Kyllene notices the change by itself, no restart needed.

Hatch your own pet with AI

Hatch a new one… in Settings › Pet (or Ctrl+K › Pet: hatch a new one…) lets AI draw a brand-new pet:

  1. Provider: pick the Image provider (the ones Hermes has set up that can draw from a reference image, for example ChatGPT (subscription), OpenAI (API key), OpenRouter, Nous Portal) and, where the provider lets you, the Image model. The dialog says up front what it costs: paid per image, or counted against your subscription. Without a suitable provider it lists what to set up first.
  2. Description: describe the pet, for example a small green dragon with glasses and a cup of coffee, then Generate drafts.
  3. Drafts and Pick: AI draws four drafts. Pick one, give it a Pet name and, if you like, a Nature for its personality (Invent with AI suggests both). New drafts or Edit description when none fits, then Hatch.
  4. Hatching: every animation is drawn and put together. Then Show as my pet, Hatch another or Throw away.

Move the pet

  • Drag it anywhere with the mouse. Dropped over text, it moves to the nearest free spot so it never covers an answer or the box you type in. The spot is remembered across restarts.
  • Double-click puts it back in its corner (bottom right, above the box you type in).
  • The spot is remembered per computer (each one has its own screen).
  • Let the pet wander the window in Settings › Pet (off by default; palette Pet: wander the window / Pet: stop wandering) lets it stroll along free spots while the agent is at rest. As soon as the agent works, it goes back to its spot.

The pet in its own window

Shift+click the pet to pop it out into its own small window on the desktop. It stays on top even when Kyllene is hidden or behind other apps (palette Pet: into its own window). Drag that window anywhere, its spot is remembered too. Shift+click again (or Pet: back into the app window) brings it back into Kyllene.

On the desktop the pet does more:

  • Click opens a small message box (the quick capture window) that writes into the conversation you used last; the answer shows there.
  • Double-click hides the main Kyllene window, or brings it back.
  • A small bubble beside it says Working… while an agent works and Your turn when one waits for you.
  • When an answer finished while you were in another app, a letter appears next to the pet. Click it to open that conversation (with several, the newest one).

What the pet's states mean

What the pet does What it means
Stands at rest The agent is idle, you can type.
Runs The agent is working, for example using a tool (searching, reading files, running a command).
Thinks The agent is working out what to do next.
Waits The agent needs you: a question, an approval or a password. Also when another open conversation needs you, not just the one you look at.
Looks sad Something failed (a tool or the answer). Have a look at the conversation.
Waves Done, the agent finished its answer.
Jumps for joy The agent finished its whole to-do list, or you thanked it ("thanks", ❤️); hearts float up over it.

The short ones (wave, jump, failure) last a moment; then the pet goes back to rest or to work.

Mood

On top of its state the pet has a mood that follows how the work goes on this computer: failures in a row make it grumpy, then fed up, then angry; successes make it happy and excited; a long quiet spell makes it relaxed and later sleepy. Over time it returns to calm. Settings › Pet shows the current Mood under the shown pet.

You see the mood in how the pet moves (pace, a shake, a bounce, a light tint) and, when a mood arrives, in a short mark over its head: ❓ ❗ ✨ 💢 💧 ♪. The mark goes after a few seconds; only 💤 (sleepy) and ⏳ (impatient) stay while the mood lasts. The mood also sets the tone of the pet's comments. Every pet shows its moods for free; Generate moods draws its own animations for them.

Comments in a speech bubble

Now and then the pet comments on the agent's work in a speech bubble: a failed tool or test, a question for you, a finished turn. The bubble stays until you click it or the next line replaces it; hover the pet to see its last lines. Set it up in Settings › Pet › Comments:

Option What it does
Mode Off (the pet stays quiet), Prepared lines (default: free ready-made lines that fit the moment and the pet's nature) or AI lines (a model writes each line from a short summary of the moment, never the whole conversation; costs tokens, and when it fails a prepared line is shown).
Model of the lines For AI lines: Like the conversation (the model of the conversation the comment is about) or one model you pick. A pricey model shows what a line costs.
Longest silence While an agent works and the pet has said nothing for this long (2–30 min, default 5), it comments on what is going on. With nothing running it stays quiet.
Language Like the app (Kyllene's language on each computer), Čeština or English.
Comment on my messages too Off by default. The pet now and then reacts to a message you send (not to short replies or commands, at most every 3 minutes). With AI lines your message goes to the comment model.

Talk to the pet by name

Write to the pet by its name in a conversation, for example Boba, what do you think? or what do you say, Boba?. The pet answers in its bubble, and the agent leaves that reply to the pet. This works whenever Comments are not Off. Talking about the pet (Boba's animation is cute) does not count. With the brain Memory or Combination, Boba, remember … saves a note to its memory.

Personality

Each pet can have a Name and a Nature (endlessly curious, talks short and funny, hates red tests); they shape its comments. Set them when hatching, or later in the pet's ⋯ › Personality… (also on a right-click on the pet); a pet without one offers Add a personality. Invent with AI suggests both. The personality is kept in Kyllene's settings and travels with the pet to your other computers; Hermes is not changed.

Pet brain

Pet brain in Settings › Pet sets how much the shown pet knows and can do when it comments and answers. It is set per pet and travels with it; each level says what it costs.

Level What the pet can do
No memory Default. Every line stands alone and the pet remembers nothing; no extra cost.
Memory Remembers you, its own lines and what happened, has small skills and does not repeat itself; about 1,000 extra tokens per line and one cheap call a day.
Agent A whole Hermes agent with the pet's name: its own memory, skills and tools, it can look things up. Every line is a full agent turn (about 17,000 tokens).
Combination Ordinary lines through Memory; when you talk to the pet by name, the whole agent answers.

With Memory or Combination two more parts show:

  • Memory: a note the pet gets with every AI line (up to 2,000 characters). Edit it and Save, or tell the pet in a conversation (Boba, remember …). Once a day the comment model tidies it. What it knows about the user (off by default) also gives the pet the first 1,000 characters of Hermes' memories/USER.md, read only.
  • Skills: small packs of what the pet knows and jokes about (say PHP and Symfony or running). Add skill (Name, When it fits: the events it is for, optional keywords, Content) or Think one up with AI. An AI line gets at most one skill that fits.

Agent and Combination need a Hermes profile for the pet: Kyllene offers Create profile (a personality from the pet's nature, its own memory, skills and tools). The profile then shows among your agents; delete it in Hermes › Profiles. When the agent cannot answer, the pet speaks with Memory (Combination) or as with No memory (Agent).

Repair animations and animation length

Animation length in Settings › Pet (under the shown pet) sets how long each of the pet's animations plays, from 1 to 8 seconds (Normal is 1.1 s); All back to normal resets them. It is free and applies at once.

For a pet you hatched, Repair animations… (its ⋯ menu, a right-click on the pet, or Ctrl+K › Pet: repair animations…) redraws only the animations that look wrong:

  1. Every animation plays side by side. Tick the broken ones. Under each you can write What should look different (optional); its length slider here also sets how many frames the new animation gets.
  2. Pick the Image provider and Image model as when hatching (one image per animation, the cost is shown) and click Draw again.
  3. Compare Now and New, then Use or Discard each one; Apply and close saves every new animation you did not discard. Restore previous look undoes the last repair.

Generate moods

Every mood is played for free by adjusting a standard animation. Generate moods… (the pet's ⋯ menu, a right-click on the pet, or Ctrl+K › Pet: generate moods…) draws the pet's own animation for the moods you pick instead; it works for any pet, also one from the Petdex. Each mood shows free adjustment or own; Pick the missing ones ticks every mood without its own animation. Drawing is paid (one image per mood, the cost is shown). Each finished mood is saved at once, and Close (keep drawing) lets the drawing run on. Remove on a mood goes back to the free adjustment.

On your other computers

With sync on, the shown pet and its size follow you: a Petdex pet installs itself on the other computer, a hatched one travels with its drawings, so you never hatch (and pay) twice. Its personality, brain, memory, skills, own moods and animation lengths travel with it. Whether the pet is shown, where it sits on the screen and its current mood stay on each computer.

Palette commands and shortcuts

Ctrl+K offers Pet…, Pet: show / Pet: hide, Pet: into its own window / Pet: back into the app window, Pet: wander the window / Pet: stop wandering, Pet: hatch a new one…, Pet: repair animations… (hatched pets) and Pet: generate moods…. Settings › Shortcuts has a Pet group: give choosing the pet, show / hide, its own window and hatching a key of your own (Set); the mouse actions (Shift+click, click, double-click, Alt + wheel) are listed there too.

Troubleshooting

Problem What to do
No pet to be seen Check that Show the pet is on in Settings › Pet and that a pet is picked (Show).
The pet does not move Reduced motion is on in your system; the pet then stands still and shows its state with one picture.
The pet says nothing Settings › Pet › Comments › Mode is Off, or no agent is working (with nothing running it stays quiet).
AI lines sound like prepared ones The model of the lines is not connected on this computer, or the call failed; a prepared line is shown instead.
Hatching cannot start No image provider that can draw from a reference image is set up in Hermes; the dialog lists what to set up (for example an OpenRouter key in Hermes › Keys).
The pet's desktop window forgets its spot On pure Wayland (no XWayland) a window cannot place itself.
