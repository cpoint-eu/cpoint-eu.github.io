Hermes – Privacy policy

Hermes is a private tool used only by its owner on their own Google accounts.

  • Data from Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive is accessed only on the owner's request and only for the owner.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on machines controlled by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
